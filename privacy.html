Oasis Bridge Privacy Policy

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy details how Oasis Bridge handles user data collected within the TikTok platform integration.

1. Information We Collect

Profile Data: We utilize TikTok's Login Kit to securely authenticate your profile identity (Avatar, Nickname, and user credentials) to maintain your secure learning vault.

Audio Data: We collect and process short voice recordings using on-device/cloud microphone tools solely to assess oral pronunciation quality and distribute gamified diamond rewards. We do not store or monetize raw audio files for marketing purposes.

2. Data Protection & Sharing

All transactional data and asset records are safely managed using secure cloud servers. We do not share, sell, or rent your private records to third-party data firms. Ad networks (such as Pangle) may serve contextual video promotions inside the web views based on platform policies.
