Oasis Bridge Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy delineates how Oasis Bridge ("we", "us", "our") orchestrates the ingest, computational processing, and systemic retention of data metrics derived from your interactive sessions with our language learning web client integrated via the TikTok Developer Portal. We operate in strict conformity with international compliance frameworks, including the GDPR, CCPA, and UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on PDPL.

1. Data Ingestion Matrix

We restrict data collection exclusively to variables required for core functional operability and game ledger integrity:

  • TikTok Identity Kit Profiles: Upon user consent authorization via the Login Kit, we harvest open-identity data nodes including your TikTok Avatar URL, Nickname, and Unique open ID string. This parameters are strictly bound to generate your cryptographic account profile vault.
  • Dynamic Acoustic Waveforms (Audio Streams): Our learning engine temporarily captures real-time microphone audio inputs. This acoustic data is processed locally or via secure database webhooks exclusively for pronunciation scoring, syllable segmentation, and dynamic token rewards distribution.
Data Minimization Commitment: Raw biological voice recordings are volatile and treated as transient data streams. We DO NOT archive raw speech recordings long-term on our master cloud storage nodes, nor do we train third-party generative models or trade biometrics to advertising broker networks.

2. Data Encryption and Sovereign Storage Architecture

All transaction ledgers, performance score charts, and configuration matrices are safely stored in fully encrypted environments using institutional-grade AES-256 protocols. Your persistent records are hosted via secure cloud servers configured with automated firewalls, rigorous TLS encryption layers, and endpoint security monitors.

3. Token Monetization and Advertising Nodes

To deliver a zero-barrier acquisition loop for users, this app integrates the TikTok Pangle Ad Network SDK. When users execute the "Rescue Limit" action, a programmatic webhook activates a third-party incentivized video ad. These interstitial ad elements may collect localized hardware parameters, regional identifiers, and cookie hashes strictly pursuant to TikTok’s independent network policy.

4. Cross-Border Data Transfers

As a decentralized global portal, the parameters we process may be transferred and computed across cross-border cloud node mirrors (including GCC servers and global CDN distribution arcs). By engaging with our client portal, you consent to these compliant architectural transfers.

5. Data Subject Rights (Your Access and Deletion Keys)

Pursuant to international data charters, you retain full rights to query your diagnostic data records or execute a formal deletion protocol. You may invoke a complete account and data wipe anytime by forwarding an authenticated query via our in-app **Feedback (FB)** channel panel or by contacting our Data Protection Officer.
